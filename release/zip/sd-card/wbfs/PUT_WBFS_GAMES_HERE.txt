Put .wbfs game files here (plus every .wbf1, .wbf2, ... piece when a
game is split), either directly or one folder below, for example:
  wbfs/Game [RMCE01]/RMCE01.wbfs
Raw .iso copies work here too (wbfs/Game [RMCE01]/RMCE01.iso), or in games. Only FAT32 cards work.
SD game boot is experimental: this same card also holds your mods
and saves.
