{{TESTER_ROLE}} RiftWii {{VERSION}} is out. {{RELEASE_URL}}

* **Settings is easier to read**: every setting shows its value, a short line on what it does and when it takes effect. A on a choice opens its list, and there's a search button beside the version. A game's own Options work the same way.
* **Get more mods** (a Wii game's Mods page) and **Get a save** (its Saves page) download from GameBanana.
* **Manage emulated NAND > Miis**: the Wii's Miis and the emulated NAND's side by side, copied either way.
* **F-Zero GX (USA)** gets past its save page, and the **Zelda OoT / Master Quest disc** starts the game you pick.
* **About 5.7 MB more memory for games**: RiftWii's own code moved out of the way.

Please test on a real Wii: does RiftWii start, and does a game start (disc, USB, SD, GameCube)? A Project+ build? Get more mods and Get a save? The Zelda disc's menu?

Sending logs (you don't need to take out your SD card): in RiftWii go to Settings > Help > Send a problem report, scan the QR code or copy the link, and post it here.
