{{TESTER_ROLE}} RiftWii {{VERSION}} is out. {{RELEASE_URL}}

A fix for big game libraries: with hundreds of games, RiftWii could run out of memory and restart, most often while it downloaded an update or the game details. Downloads now go straight to the SD card, and covers take turns in memory.

Tried in Dolphin only so far. If you have a big library: scroll the shelf with full covers and update. Does RiftWii keep going?

Sending logs (you don't need to take out your SD card): in RiftWii go to Settings > Help > Send a problem report, scan the QR code or copy the link, and post it here with what you tested.
