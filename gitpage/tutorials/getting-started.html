Getting Started

A simple introduction to installing and opening RiftWii.

Read the official Getting Started guide
Requirements

- A Wii, or a Wii U in Wii mode, with the Homebrew Channel
- An SD Card that is formatted to FAT32
- A USB Drive formatted to FAT32, NTFS, or as a WBFS drive by a WBFS manager (Optional)
- A computer or device to download RiftWii

Step 1

Download the zip file from the Github.

Latest Version of RiftWii
Step 2

Copy the contents of the sd-card folder onto the root of your SD Card. It should look like: 

SD Card
├── apps/
│   ├── riftwii/
│   └── riftwii_channel/
├── games/
├── riftwii/
│   └── themes/
├── riivolution/
└── wbfs/

If you have a USB Drive, also copy the contents of usb-drive onto your USB Drive. It should look like: 

USB Drive
├── games/
└── wbfs/

Launching RiftWii

RiftWii is all setup now! You can now launch it via the Homebrew Channel!

Load RiftWii from the Wii Menu

Simply open the RiftWii Channel Installer, and press A to install!

All Done!
